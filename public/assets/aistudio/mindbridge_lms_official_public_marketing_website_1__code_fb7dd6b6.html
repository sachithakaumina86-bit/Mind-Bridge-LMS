Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Common Operations Questions

How does the student login work across multiple different institutes? expand_more

A student registers once on MindBridge. When they enroll in your courses, they access your isolated portal. Through the unified Tenant Switcher, they can effortlessly toggle between different educators without logging out or juggling duplicate passwords.

How does manual LKR bank transfer slip verification operate? expand_more

Students submit payment receipts via image upload (BOC, Sampath, Commercial Bank, CDM deposits, or online banking slips). Your admin dashboard highlights unverified deposits, allowing your finance team to approve access or request clarification in seconds.

Is Sinhala Unicode rendering supported across exams and notes? expand_more

Yes, completely. The typography system includes Noto Sans Sinhala natively. Sinhala questions, mathematical formulas, and explanations render cleanly with zero font clipping across mobile and desktop screens.

Do I need my own Google Gemini API key for the AI assistant? expand_more

MindBridge features a Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) model. You can plug in your Google Gemini AI key directly into your tenant settings, granting you total control over usage limits, costs, and customized prompt parameters.

Can I add multiple staff members to help check bank slips? expand_more

Yes. The standard tenant subscription permits up to two dedicated staff members baseline who can be assigned restricted roles (such as Slip Verifier or Question Bank Editor) without exposing your full business analytics.
