Apex Exam Tip • 2025 Paper Analysis

Remember: When solving partial fractions with constants K, do NOT treat K as a variable. Pull 1/K out of the integration symbol before taking natural logarithms to secure all 15 method marks!

Rigorous Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivations

Official syllabus derivation notation for G.C.E. A/L Combined Mathematics Paper I

Step 1: The General Logistic Growth Differential Form Eq 34.1a
\[ \frac{dP}{dt} = k P \left(1 - \frac{P}{K}\right) = \frac{k}{K} P (K - P) \]

Here, \(P(t)\) denotes the population at time \(t\), \(k > 0\) is the intrinsic growth rate parameter, and \(K\) is the carrying capacity of the environment.

Step 2: Separation of Variables and Partial Fractions Eq 34.1b
\[ \int \frac{dP}{P(K - P)} = \int \frac{k}{K} dt \]
\[ \frac{1}{K} \int \left( \frac{1}{P} + \frac{1}{K - P} \right) dP = \frac{k}{K} \int dt \]

Multiplying both sides by \(K\) eliminates the constant multiplier:

\[ \ln|P| - \ln|K - P| = kt + C \implies \ln \left| \frac{P}{K - P} \right| = kt + C \]
Step 3: Boundary Condition Application & Explicit Solution Final Closed Form
\[ P(t) = \frac{K}{1 + \left( \frac{K - P_0}{P_0} \right) e^{-kt}} \]
check_circle As \(t \to \infty\), \(e^{-kt} \to 0\), leading naturally to \(P(t) \to K\), establishing the asymptotic ceiling.

How do we resolve the partial fractions \(\frac{1}{P(K-P)}\) quickly during exam pressure?
auto_awesome Gemini Academic Solver • Just now

Here is the quick Cover-Up Rule method for resolving \(\frac{1}{P(K-P)}\):

\[ \frac{1}{P(K-P)} = \frac{1}{K}\left( \frac{1}{P} + \frac{1}{K-P} \right) \]

Sinhala Explanation (සිංහලෙන්):
මෙහිදී ලවයේ \(1\) වෙනුවට \(\frac{(K - P) + P}{K}\) යොදාගැනීමෙන් තත්පර 10කින් අර්ධ භාග වෙන් කළ හැක:

\(\frac{1}{K} \left[ \frac{K-P}{P(K-P)} + \frac{P}{P(K-P)} \right]\)
\(= \frac{1}{K}\left( \frac{1}{P} + \frac{1}{K-P} \right)\)
